ROP-Sahayak: Master Architectural Notes & Brainstorming

          ICROP-3 Standards
        

        

          ROP-Sahayak bridges the critical shortage of pediatric retina specialists across tier-2 and rural NICUs through a dual approach:
        

        

          

            
1. Deterministic Biomarkers (No Black Box)

            

              Clinicians demand mathematical explainability. The engine computes Arteriolar Tortuosity Index (ATI) and Venular Dilation Index (VDI) directly from vessel skeletons, preventing black-box hallucinations.
            

          

          

            
2. Longitudinal Care & Loss-to-Follow-Up

            

              ROP is a temporal disease. Combining multi-visit delta tracking with systemic factors (WINROP/CHOP-ROP) and automated SMS/WhatsApp alerts ensures zero premature infants go blind due to missed follow-ups.
